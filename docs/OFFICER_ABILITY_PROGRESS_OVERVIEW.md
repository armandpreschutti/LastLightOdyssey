## Officer Progression Table

---

### 1. The Captain
> **Role:** Managing the squad’s action economy and finishing weak enemies.

* **Level 1 (Base): Execute**
    * **Effect:** Guaranteed kill on any enemy within 4 tiles that is below 50% HP. Never misses. 2-turn cooldown.
* **Level 2 (Tactical Choice)**
    * **Option A: Lead by Example** (Passive): When the Captain kills an enemy, all other squad members gain **+1 AP** on their next turn.
    * **Option B: Coordinate Fire** (Active - 1 AP): Mark a target. All allies gain **+20% Accuracy** and **+25% Critical Chance** against that target for 1 turn.
* **Level 3 (Mastery Choice)**
    * **Option A: Warlord** (Passive): Execute no longer has a cooldown. If you have enough AP, you can chain multiple executions in a single turn.
    * **Option B: No One Left Behind** (Passive): If an ally within 5 tiles takes lethal damage, they survive with **1 HP** and become immune to all damage for 1 turn. (Triggers once per mission per ally).
    * **Option C: Command Presence** (Passive Aura): All allies within 4 tiles of the Captain gain **+10% Accuracy** and **+15% Critical Chance**. The Captain gains **+10 Defense** for every ally within this aura.

---

### 2. The Scout
> **Role:** Map control, high mobility, and reaction fire.

* **Level 1 (Base): Overwatch**
    * **Effect:** Take a reaction shot at the first enemy that moves in Line of Sight. Guaranteed hit. 2-turn cooldown.
* **Level 2 (Tactical Choice)**
    * **Option A: Hit & Run** (Passive): If the Scout moves and shoots in the same turn, they gain **+3 Movement Points** immediately to retreat or reposition.
    * **Option B: Deep Scanner** (Active - 0 AP): Reveals all enemies in a large radius (15 tiles) for 1 turn, even through walls. Cooldown: 3 turns.
* **Level 3 (Mastery Choice)**
    * **Option A: Killzone** (Upgrade): Overwatch now triggers on every enemy that moves in your Line of Sight during the enemy turn, not just the first one. (Consumes ammo for each shot).
    * **Option B: Phantom** (Active - 1 AP): Become **Invisible** for 2 turns. Enemies cannot target you. Your first attack from invisibility deals **+100% Damage** but reveals you.
    * **Option C: Untouchable** (Passive): If you kill an enemy during your turn, the next attack against you during the enemy turn is guaranteed to **Miss**.

---

### 3. The Tech
> **Role:** Area denial, hacking, and robotic support.

* **Level 1 (Base): Turret**
    * **Effect:** Deploy an auto-firing sentry on an adjacent tile (15 DMG, 6 range). Lasts 3 turns. 2-turn cooldown.
* **Level 2 (Tactical Choice)**
    * **Option A: Combat Engineer** (Passive): Turrets now gain **Shielding** (20 HP barrier) and their duration is increased to 5 turns.
    * **Option B: Sapper** (Active - 1 AP): Throw a localized EMP grenade. Deals minimal damage but **Stuns robotic enemies** and disables enemy weapons in a 3x3 area for 1 turn.
* **Level 3 (Mastery Choice)**
    * **Option A: Twin-Link** (Passive): You can now have 2 Turrets active at the same time. Deploying a second turret does not destroy the first one.
    * **Option B: Overclock** (Active - 0 AP): Target your active Turret. It immediately fires 3 times at random targets in range, then self-destructs (dealing 20 Area Damage to adjacent tiles).
    * **Option C: Haywire Protocol** (Active - 1 AP): Hack a robotic enemy (Turret or Mech). The enemy fights for you for 2 turns, then shuts down (Stunned) for 1 turn. Cooldown: 4 turns.

---

### 4. The Medic
> **Role:** Healing, buffing, and keeping the squad fighting.

* **Level 1 (Base): Patch**
    * **Effect:** Heal yourself or an ally within 3 tiles for **62.5% Max HP**. 2-turn cooldown.
* **Level 2 (Tactical Choice)**
    * **Option A: Adrenaline Patch** (Upgrade): Patch now also grants the target **+2 Movement** and **+15% Accuracy** for 2 turns.
    * **Option B: Field Surgeon** (Passive): Medics automatically stabilize any ally who drops to 0 HP within 4 tiles, preventing death (unless the Medic is also killed).
* **Level 3 (Mastery Choice)**
    * **Option A: Miracle Worker** (Active - 2 AP): **Global Heal**. Instantly heal all squad members on the map for 50% HP, regardless of distance or Line of Sight. (One use per mission).
    * **Option B: Toxicologist** (Passive): Your attacks apply **Poison** (5 DMG per turn, -20% Aim) to enemies. Your Patch ability now removes all status effects from allies.
    * **Option C: Stim Injector** (Active - 1 AP): Inject an ally. They gain **+2 AP** immediately and take **-50% Damage** for 1 turn. Cooldown: 3 turns.

---

### 5. The Heavy
> **Role:** Tanking, cover destruction, and close-range burst.

* **Level 1 (Base): Charge**
    * **Effect:** Rush an enemy within 4 tiles. Instant-kills basic enemies; deals **2x Base Damage** to heavy enemies. 2-turn cooldown.
* **Level 2 (Tactical Choice)**
    * **Option A: Bulldozer** (Passive): Charge now destroys all cover and obstacles in the path. You gain **+20 Armor** after charging.
    * **Option B: Suppression Fire** (Active - 2 AP): Target a cone area. Deal light damage to all enemies in the area and remove their ability to move next turn (**Pin Down**).
* **Level 3 (Mastery Choice)**
    * **Option A: Juggernaut** (Passive): You are immune to Critical Hits and Knockback. If you end your turn without attacking, you regenerate **15% Max HP**.
    * **Option B: Rocket Salvo** (Active - 2 AP): Launch a micro-missile swarm at a target area (3x3 grid). Deals **40 Area Damage** and destroys all cover/obstacles in the zone. Cooldown: 3 turns.
    * **Option C: Intimidate** (Passive Aura): Enemies within 3 tiles of the Heavy suffer **-20% Accuracy** and cannot use Overwatch or Reaction Fire.

---

### 6. The Sniper
> **Role:** Long-range elimination and zone control.

* **Level 1 (Base): Precision Shot**
    * **Effect:** Guaranteed hit on any visible enemy. Deals **2x Base Damage**. 2-turn cooldown.
* **Level 2 (Tactical Choice)**
    * **Option A: Damn Good Ground** (Passive): If you have not moved this turn, gain **+15% Critical Chance** and **+2 Sight Range**.
    * **Option B: Snap Shot** (Passive): Precision Shot costs only **1 AP** (instead of ending turn), allowing you to move and shoot or shoot twice.
* **Level 3 (Mastery Choice)**
    * **Option A: Serial** (Passive): If you kill an enemy with your main weapon, your **Action Points are fully refunded**. (Allows chaining kills until you miss or run out of targets).
    * **Option B: Apex Predator** (Passive): You deal **+100% Damage** against enemies that are at Full Health. (Designed to one-shot high-value targets).
    * **Option C: Double Tap** (Active - 1 AP): Fire two standard shots at the same target with a -15% Accuracy penalty on the second shot. Cooldown: 2 turns.
